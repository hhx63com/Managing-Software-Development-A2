Introduction and Types of Elemental Reactions

In Genshin Impact, elemental reaction is one of the core mechanisms of combat system. By combining different elements, players can trigger various powerful effects.

The following are all reaction types.

Melt: Cryo + Pyro

Introduction: Melting reaction will greatly enhance the damage of fire element or ice element.

Superconduct: Cryo + Electro

Introduction: Superconducting reaction will reduce the physical resistance of the target and cause damage to the range of ice elements.

Frozen: Cryo + Hydro

Introduction: make the target unable to move, and trigger "broken ice" after being hit hard, causing physical damage.

Vaporize: Pyro + Hydro

Introduction: Evaporation reaction will greatly enhance the damage of fire or water.

Overloaded: Pyro + Electro

Introduction: Causing an explosion in the range of fire elements and flying small enemies.

Electro-Charged: Electro + Hydro

Introduction: the inductive reaction will continue to cause lightning damage and continue to affect the surrounding enemies.

Burning: Pyro + Dendro

Introduction: Fire element damage is caused continuously, and the residual grass element can be ignited again.

Quicken: Dendro + Electro

Introduction: Trigger the "Catalyze" state, and improve the subsequent damage of thunder or grass elements.

Aggravate: Dendro + Electro + Electro

Introduction: When "Catalyze", based on the role level and element mastery, improve the damage of thunder elements.

Spread: Dendro + Electro + Dendro

Introduction: When "Catalyze", based on the role level and element mastery, improve the damage of thunder elements.

Bloom: Dendro + Hydro

Introduction: "Dendro Core" is generated, and it explodes after 6 seconds, causing damage to grass element range.

Hyperbloom: Dendro + Hydro + Electro(Need to be in order)

Introduction: "Dendro Core" is transformed into "Sprawling Shots" to track the enemy, causing grass element damage.

Burgeon: Dendro + Hydro + Pyro(Need to be in order)

Introduction: "Dendro Core" immediately exploded, causing a higher range of grass element damage.

Swirl: Anemo + Other Elements

Introduction: spread the current element to the surrounding enemies, triggering the damage of range elements.

Crystallize: Geo + Other Elements

Introduction: Generate shields of corresponding elements to absorb the damage of specific elements.
